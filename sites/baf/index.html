Inside the facilitation engine

From a visitor's answer to a financed order.

Capture Every visitor's need is logged — QR form, printed objective form at the info desk, or an exhibitor marshal typing into the live site's questionnaire.
Structure Answers become a demand log entry: product, quantity, budget, district, timing, and the exhibitor who can serve it.
Secure Credit needs are matched to a supplier entry and covered by a creditor or guarantor, so risk sits on a named, traceable record.
Return Orders, repeat buyers and paid listings feed the directory, the outreach programme and the network's recurring revenue.

Admin backend, dashboards and analytics are specified in the network blueprint; the public forms are live on the expo site.
