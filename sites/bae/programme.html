● Annual fixture — 4 to 8 November 2026

An initiative of Busolya Agro Farmers Ltd

4–8 November 2026

Programme

Five days, one rhythm: the exhibition runs all day, the classroom runs in sessions, and the registry desk never closes. This is the draft running order published for planning; confirmations are marked as they land.

Draft running order. Session times, speakers and the venue are confirmed with the host and published here before print. Exhibitors can request a stand slot through the registration form.
Day 1 — Wed 4 Nov

Opening & the registry desk

  • MorningGates open. Exhibitor registration and exhibitor-log entry completed before stands are opened to visitors.
  • MiddayOpening ceremony with clan and Kingdom representation; the expo declared open.
  • AfternoonInformation desk and QR forms live. First visitor-requests entries of the season.
  • All dayFarm Aid clinic opens — land holders and intending managers booked in for the week.
Day 2 — Thu 5 Nov

Farm Aid & farm accountancy

  • Session 1Farm Aid: managing a farm — the manager's job, husbandry, labour, supervision and reporting.
  • Session 2Farm accountancy — the daily book, the stock book, the season summary; how proceeds are shared on recorded numbers.
  • Session 3Credit, savings & guarantors — how a SACCO reads a member and what a guarantor actually signs.
  • All dayCredit desk open: creditor and guarantor records attached to visitor-requests entries.
Day 3 — Fri 6 Nov

Culture, clans & land

  • Session 1Abasolya, Bataka & the layers of a clan — ekika, essiga, omutuba, olunyiriri, oluggya, enju; the council and the Katikkiro at each layer.
  • Session 2Land, tenure & dues — mailo, kibanja and busuulu, and what must be in a farm or tenancy agreement.
  • Session 3Agronomy clinic — cassava, banana, coffee and maize: planting material, spacing, pests, seasonal decisions.
  • All dayLand desk: dormant plots offered to Farm Aid, agreements reviewed before signing.
Day 4 — Sat 7 Nov

Market day & skilling

  • Session 1Livestock & poultry — feeds, housing, disease control, breeding and the records that show a herd growing.
  • Session 2Post-harvest & standards — drying, storage, grading, packaging, and what a buyer checks before paying.
  • Session 3Digital & market access — QR forms, the online directory, WhatsApp selling, being found outside your district.
  • AfternoonBuyer-exhibitor matchmaking run off the visitor requests, not off the room.
Day 5 — Sun 8 Nov

Results & the year ahead

  • MorningVisitor-requests results read out: what visitors asked for, and which exhibitors can serve it.
  • MiddayFarm Aid cohort intake; directory listings confirmed; network membership opened for the year.
  • AfternoonClosing ceremony and handover to the outreach programme.
