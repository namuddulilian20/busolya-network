Three routes, one record

The expo questionnaire

Scan, print, or log at the stand. Every answer lands in the demand log; every exhibitor lands in the suppliers log; credit sits on a named creditor or guarantor.

Demand log entry

Two minutes. Name and phone are the only things you must write.

Credit & guarantor

What happens to this

  1. 1. It becomes a numbered demand entry.
  2. 2. It is matched to a suppliers-log entry by category, quantity and district.
  3. 3. Credit, if needed, is attached to a named guarantor, not to a handshake.
  4. 4. The match is followed up through the outreach programme.

Privacy

Name, phone and need are used to match you with a supplier and to follow up. They are not sold. Ask the desk to remove your entry at any time.

Suppliers log entry

For exhibitors, service providers and anyone who supplies the network all year.

What a listing buys you

  • ✓ A record that survives the expo
  • ✓ Matching against every demand-log entry in your category
  • ✓ A credit history once your orders clear
  • ✓ Print and online directory placement

Stand credit

Exhibitors who need help with stand costs can ask Abataka Mutindo SACCO — credit is assessed against a member record, not a promise.

Why this form exists

A visitor who cannot fill a web form still has an order. The tick sheet keeps that order in the log and keeps the queue moving.
