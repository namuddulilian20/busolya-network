Three short forms — visitor request, supply, linkage

The registry desk

Make a request

Five to seven questions, asked once, answered in a minute. If you came to buy or need something, use the visitor request form. Suppliers register what they offer, and exhibitors propose a partnership. Every answer reaches the registry desk.

Your details

Visitor requests

What visitors asked for, by product, volume and timing. The desk matches each request against the suppliers log.

Suppliers log

Capacity, price band and delivery range, so a lead goes to a supplier who can actually deliver.

Linkage board

Who wants to meet whom, with the deal on the table. Closed linkages become directory entries after verification.
