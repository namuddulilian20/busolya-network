What happens on site

Exhibition, classroom and registry desk

The exhibition

Inputs, seed, feed, machinery, livestock services, processing, finance and crafts. Every stand is registered on the suppliers log before the gates open.

The classroom

Nine teaching subjects, anchored by Farm Aid: managing a farm and keeping a farm's accounts. Culture, land and credit are taught beside agronomy.

The registry desk

QR forms, printed objective forms at the information desk, and marshals logging at the stand. Every answer becomes a demand or supply record.

Three ways in

How a visitor gets captured

Scan the QR code Opens the contextualised questionnaire on your own phone. No app, no queue. Works on any smart phone with a camera.
Fill the printed form Tick boxes, no writing required beyond a name and a number. Filled at the information desk and entered same day.
Talk to the marshal Each exhibitor marshal logs the lead at the stand on the live site, so no order walks out of the gate unrecorded.
Open the questionnaire Print the desk forms

Anchor subject

Farm Aid clinic

How to prepare a manager, how to run a farm, how to keep a farm's accounts. Land holders bring a plot; farmers bring a record of what they have managed.
